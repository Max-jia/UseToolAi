## The 10

### ChatGPT — Best All-Around Assistant

**The pitch:** One subscription covering chat, image generation, video, and coding.

**What it does well:** Broadest feature set of any assistant; Plus has held at $20 since 2023.

**Where it falls short:** Free tier is capped and ad-supported; Pro tiers cost $100–200.

**Price:** Free / Go $8/mo / Plus $20/mo. **Get this if:** you want one tool for everything. **Skip if:** you need an assistant inside Google Workspace.

### Claude — Best for Coding and Writing

**The pitch:** The sharpest reasoning model, plus Claude Code for agentic programming.

**What it does well:** Leads coding benchmarks; writes the least AI-sounding long-form text.

**Where it falls short:** No image or video generation in the app.

**Price:** Free / Pro $20/mo / Max $100–200. **Get this if:** you write code or long documents daily. **Skip if:** you need media generation in the same tool.

### Cursor — Best AI Coding IDE

**The pitch:** The gold-standard AI editor — predictive tab completion plus multi-file agentic refactoring.

**What it does well:** Deep codebase understanding; cloud agents and code review built in.

**Where it falls short:** Pricing structure changed in 2026 (Individual tiers consolidated at $20); heavy agent use burns through limits.

**Price:** Free / $20/mo Individual / $40/user Teams. **Get this if:** you code professionally. **Skip if:** you want an assistant, not an IDE.

### Midjourney — Best Artistic Image Generation

**The pitch:** The most visually distinctive image model — and the most consistently priced tool on this list.

**What it does well:** Unmatched artistic quality; pricing stable at $10/$30/$60/$120 for years.

**Where it falls short:** No free tier; text-in-image rendering trails GPT Image 2.

**Price:** $10/mo Basic / $30 Standard / $60 Pro / $120 Mega. **Get this if:** image quality is everything. **Skip if:** you're on a zero budget.

### ElevenLabs — Best Text-to-Speech

**The pitch:** The highest-quality AI voices and the industry-standard voice cloning.

**What it does well:** Natural voices across 60+ languages; generous free tier; API for developers.

**Where it falls short:** Starter rose to $6/mo in 2026; Scale dropped to $299 — pricing has been in motion.

**Price:** Free / $6 Starter / $22 Creator / $99 Pro / $299 Scale. **Get this if:** you produce voice content. **Skip if:** you just need occasional narration.

### Notion AI — Best AI Knowledge Base

**The pitch:** AI baked into your notes, docs, and project management — and now included, not sold separately.

**What it does well:** The AI add-on was eliminated in 2025; full AI ships inside Business. Ask Notion and AI agents work on your actual content.

**Where it falls short:** Free and Plus tiers only get a limited trial — full AI requires Business.

**Price:** $20/user/mo Business (annual). **Get this if:** your team already lives in Notion. **Skip if:** you don't use Notion.

### Zapier — Best No-Code Automation

**The pitch:** Connect 7,000+ apps without code — still the default automation platform.

**What it does well:** Deepest app catalog; AI agents on top of automations; free tier for testing.

**Where it falls short:** Team pricing moved to $69/mo (annual) — the old $99 rate was cut, but task limits still bite.

**Price:** Free / $19.99 Starter / $49 Professional / $69 Team. **Get this if:** you need integrations nothing else has. **Skip if:** you only need a few simple automations (Make is cheaper).

### Canva AI — Best Design for Non-Designers

**The pitch:** AI image generation, background removal, and magic resize inside the design tool everyone already uses.

**What it does well:** Magic Studio tools are genuinely useful; Teams pricing dropped to $10/user (annual).

**Where it falls short:** AI output can be generic; heavy AI use hits credit limits.

**Price:** Free / $15/mo Pro / $10/user Teams (annual). **Get this if:** you design occasionally but professionally. **Skip if:** you need pixel-level control (use Figma).

### HeyGen — Best AI Video Avatars

**The pitch:** Realistic AI avatars for talking-head videos — marketing, training, and localization without a camera.

**What it does well:** 175+ languages; Studio-quality avatars; credit system keeps costs predictable.

**Where it falls short:** Pro pricing varies by source ($49–99/mo) — verify before subscribing; credits don't roll over.

**Price:** Free / $24/mo Creator (annual). **Get this if:** you produce training or marketing videos at scale. **Skip if:** you just need simple video editing.

### Perplexity — Best AI Search

**The pitch:** Answers with citations — every claim links to a source you can check.

**What it does well:** The most citation-transparent AI search; free tier is genuinely usable.

**Where it falls short:** Enterprise pricing rose to $40/seat in 2026; free daily caps exist.

**Price:** Free / $20/mo Pro / $200 Max. **Get this if:** you want search you can verify. **Skip if:** you want a full assistant, not a search engine.
