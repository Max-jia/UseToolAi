If you checked the pricing of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini more than a year ago, your information is wrong. All three restructured their plans since then — new tiers, renamed tiers, discontinued tiers. We re-verified every price against the official pricing pages in August 2026. Here is the current state, and who should pay for what.

## What Changed Since 2025

**Gemini restructured everything** (May 2026, Google I/O). The old "Advanced" plan is gone. The ladder is now AI Plus ($7.99), AI Pro ($19.99), AI Ultra ($99.99), and Ultra 20x ($199.99), with compute-based usage limits replacing message counts.

**ChatGPT added a budget tier.** Go ($8/mo) launched January 2026 — roughly 10x the free tier's message volume, still ad-supported. Plus ($20) and Pro ($100/$200) are unchanged.

**Claude kept its ladder but tightened the free tier.** Pro is $20 ($17 annually), Max is $100 or $200, and Claude Code is now only in paid plans — the free tier no longer includes it.

## ChatGPT — Best All-Around Value

**The pitch:** The most complete assistant: web search, image generation (GPT Image 2), video (Sora), code (Codex), and agent mode in one subscription.

**What it does well:**
- Broadest feature set of the three — you rarely need a second tool
- Plus at $20 has held its price since February 2023
- Go at $8 is the cheapest real paid tier of the three

**Where it falls short:**
- Free tier has ads (US) and a hard message cap
- Pro tiers ($100/$200) are priced for power users only

**Price:** Free (capped, ads) / Go $8/mo / Plus $20/mo / Pro $100–200/mo

**Get this if:** You want one subscription that covers chat, images, video, and coding.

**Skip this if:** You live in Google Workspace and want the assistant inside your email and docs.

## Claude — Best for Coding and Long-Form Writing

**The pitch:** The strongest reasoning and writing model of the three, plus Claude Code — an agentic coding tool that writes multi-file changes.

**What it does well:**
- Industry-leading coding benchmarks (SWE-bench) and the least "AI-sounding" long-form writing
- Pro includes Claude Code, which rivals standalone coding tools
- 1M-token context on Max tiers — entire codebases in one session

**Where it falls short:**
- No image/video generation in the consumer app
- The free tier is now chat-only — no Claude Code
- Team seats are pricey ($125/seat for the premium tier)

**Price:** Free (chat only) / Pro $20/mo ($17 annual) / Max $100–200/mo

**Get this if:** You write code or long documents daily and want the sharpest reasoning.

**Skip this if:** You need one tool for everything, including image generation.

## Gemini — Best for Google Users, Cheapest Entry

**The pitch:** Native Google Workspace integration — searches your Gmail, Drive, and Calendar — plus the cheapest paid entry of the three.

**What it does well:**
- AI Plus at $7.99 is the cheapest paid tier anywhere
- Deep Workspace integration no competitor matches
- Ultra tiers bundle YouTube Premium and Google Home perks

**Where it falls short:**
- The free tier's compute-based usage limits are confusing (limits refresh every 5 hours)
- Quality of long-form writing trails Claude
- The 2026 restructure renamed plans out from under existing subscribers

**Price:** Free / AI Plus $7.99/mo / AI Pro $19.99/mo / AI Ultra $99.99/mo / Ultra 20x $199.99/mo

**Get this if:** You live in Google Workspace and want AI inside your existing apps.

**Skip this if:** Your main use is coding — Claude is stronger for the same money.

## The Bottom Line

All three are $20/month at the tier that matters — the difference is what you get for it. Pick ChatGPT for breadth, Claude for depth, and Gemini if your life already runs on Google.
