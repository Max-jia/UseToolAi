Synthesia answers a specific question: how do you make a professional-looking video without a camera, a crew, or a reshoot budget? You type a script, pick one of 140+ avatars, and the platform speaks it in your language with synced lips. The output works for training, onboarding, and product explainers. Within that use case Synthesia is the enterprise default, with 50,000+ companies using it.

What we would tell a friend before signing up: the free plan gives one minute per month, which is a demo, not a trial. Real use starts at $29 a month. And while the avatars keep improving, they still look slightly uncanny up close, so a video of a real person still reads as more human. Buy it for what it is: consistent, scalable, camera-free video, at the cost of some warmth. The custom avatar tier, which turns executives into presenters, only unlocks on Enterprise, a fact worth knowing before you fall in love with the demo. Pricing checked August 4, 2026.
