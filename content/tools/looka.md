Looka is for the moment every founder knows: you have a business name, a vague color preference, and no designer budget. You answer a few questions, the generator returns hundreds of logo options, and you pick one to refine. At $20 one-time for the logo file, it is the cheapest way to look credible on day one. The Brand Kit add-on ($96 a year) spins that same logo into business cards, social headers, and letterhead.

The trade-offs are worth stating plainly, because the card keeps them on the table. AI-generated logos trend generic, and nothing in the process guarantees a mark that stands out from the next startup's. You own the file, not exclusive rights to the design, so anything you plan to trademark still deserves a human designer. For an MVP, a side project, or a shop that just needs to look professional fast, that trade usually pays off. Pricing checked August 8, 2026.
