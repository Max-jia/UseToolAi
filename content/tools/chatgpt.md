ChatGPT is the default AI assistant for good reasons: 300 million people use it weekly, and it's the only subscription that bundles chat, image generation, coding, and research in one place. If you only pay for one AI tool, this is usually the one.

The pricing ladder is wide — seven tiers now. The Plus plan at $20 a month is the sweet spot, and it has held that price for over three years while adding major features. The free tier started showing ads in the US in February 2026, and a new $8 Go tier sits between free and Plus with higher limits but ads still included. The Pro tiers ($100-200) exist for people who burn through Plus limits every month; most users never need them.

The real complaints are consistent across review sites: occasional hallucinations that need checking, a pricing structure that's grown complicated, and generic writing if you don't prompt carefully. It's the broadest tool, not always the best at any one thing — Claude reads long documents better, Perplexity handles citation-heavy research, and DeepSeek covers the budget crowd. But for general-purpose AI help, nothing else packs this much into one subscription. Pricing checked August 5, 2026.
